**No stock photos are used at launch.** If you add any:

1. Buy or download them from a source that grants a commercial licence (record the licence type, e.g. "Standard commercial", and its limits).
2. Save the licence or receipt in the company drive and link it in the *Proof* column.
3. Do not use images of identifiable people without a model release.
4. Bus company logos are used only with that company's written permission, as part of its partnership agreement. Add each one here with the date permission was given.
5. Every `<img>` needs meaningful `alt` text, or `alt=""` if it is purely decorative.
