# Compliance checklist

How each launch requirement is met, where to find it in the code, and what still needs a human decision. Status key: **Done** means built and tested in code. **Your action** means something the code can't do for you (a signature, a fact, a legal review).

> I'm not a lawyer. The policies are drafted for Ethiopia's Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024 and general consumer-protection practice. Have an Ethiopian lawyer review them before launch.

## Your actions before launch

1. **Business details:** set `BUSINESS_LEGAL_NAME`, `BUSINESS_TIN`, `BUSINESS_TRADE_LICENSE`, `BUSINESS_ADDRESS`, `BUSINESS_PHONE` and the support emails in `api/.env`.
2. **Register as a data controller** with the Ethiopian Communications Authority (required by Proclamation 1321/2024).
3. **Legal review** of the four policies by an Ethiopian lawyer, then an **Amharic translation** by a qualified translator (save it to `api/resources/legal/am/*.md`; the API serves it automatically).
4. **Confirm the policy choices** written into the Terms and Refund Policy:
   - full refund when a trip is delayed more than 3 hours;
   - service fee not refunded when the customer cancels;
   - 2-year booking history and 10-year financial retention.
5. **Partner agreements:** activate an operator in admin and record `partnership_signed` only after the contract is signed. Get written permission for each logo.
6. **Processor contracts / DPAs** with Chapa, telebirr, verify.et, your SMS gateway, email provider and host. Check where the host stores data (rules on transfers abroad).
7. **App store forms:** fill the Google Play Data safety form and Apple App Privacy labels to match `docs/THIRD_PARTY_SDKS.md` and the iOS privacy manifest.
8. **Accessibility test with people:** a short session with a screen-reader user (TalkBack/VoiceOver) and keyboard-only on the website.
